# MSAP Changelog

User-facing updates and improvements for the **MMSI Sales & Accounting Program (MSAP)**.

---

## [v1.1.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added
- **What's New popup:** View the latest MSAP updates automatically on the dashboard after a release, or reopen them anytime from the user menu.
- **Full-Screen Calendar Range Selector:** Pick single days or multi-month ranges (up to 1 year) across **1 Day**, **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, and **1Y** views with desktop drag, controlled **Start / End** selection, and color-coded status indicators without full-page reloads.
- **Operational Triage & Schedule Detail Modal:** Filter Vessel Schedules by **Conflicts**, **Needs Tugs**, **Pending Review**, or **Missing Tickets**, and click any schedule or tugboat assignment to open a screenshot-ready Schedule Detail modal.

### Changed
- **Vessel Schedules Day-Columns & Tugboat Matrix:** Replaced the permanent calendar sidebar with full-height horizontally scrollable day columns (up to 15 days) and a compact vertical list for longer ranges, added inline **Schedule Conflict** dividers between conflicting schedules, folded search and filters out of the way, and upgraded the **Tugboats** view to a readable Tugboat × Day matrix on desktop/laptop and borderless stacked rows on mobile.
- **Schedule Form Polish:** Added quick duration pills (`+1h` to `+6h`), board filter pre-fill, and a live Free vs. Booked tugboat availability panel with one-click assignment.
